Somincola LLC (“Somincola”, “we”, “us”) builds software with privacy as a default, not an afterthought. This policy explains what information we handle on **somincola.com** (the “Website”) and how we treat it. Individual products, such as Moro for Mastodon, may publish their own privacy policies with product-specific details; where they do, those policies apply to that product.

## Summary

- We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not show advertising.
- The Website uses no third-party analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies.
- We collect only what you choose to send us, such as a message through our contact form.

## Information we collect

**Information you provide.** When you contact us by email or through the contact form, we receive your name, email address and the contents of your message. We use this solely to respond to you and keep a record of our correspondence.

**Technical information.** Like all websites, our hosting and content delivery provider (Cloudflare) processes basic technical data — such as IP address, browser type and the page requested — in order to deliver pages securely and protect against abuse. We do not use this data to build profiles of visitors.

**Purchases.** If you buy a product or service from us directly, payments are processed by Stripe, Inc. We never see or store your full card details. Purchases made inside our apps through the Apple App Store are processed by Apple under Apple’s terms and privacy policy; we receive only the limited information Apple provides to developers.

## How we use information

We use information only to respond to enquiries and provide support; deliver, operate, secure and improve our products and services; process payments and meet tax, accounting and other legal obligations; and prevent fraud and abuse.

## Service providers

We share information only with service providers that help us run our business, under agreements that limit their use of it — for example Cloudflare (hosting and security), Resend (delivering contact-form email), Stripe (payments) and Apple (app distribution). We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights and users.

## Cookies

The Website does not set tracking or advertising cookies. Our infrastructure provider may set strictly necessary cookies used for security and performance.

## Retention

We keep correspondence for as long as needed to help you and for a reasonable period afterward, and keep transaction records for as long as the law requires. You can ask us to delete your correspondence at any time.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live — including under the GDPR and US state privacy laws such as the CCPA — you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, email [contact@somincola.com](mailto:contact@somincola.com). We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

## International transfers

Somincola is based in the United States and works with providers that may process data in other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

## Children

Our Website and services are not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures — including encryption in transit — to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work hard to protect what you share with us.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
